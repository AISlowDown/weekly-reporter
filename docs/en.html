04 / FAQ

A few things to know

Can I use it without Computer History?

Yes. Supply activity logs or work notes instead. The report identifies its sources. This skill does not include a recorder or enable recording itself.

Are the durations exact working hours?

Only reliable time logs support verified durations. With your agreement, timestamped records can support activity-window estimates, explicitly labeled as such. Missing records do not mean no work.

How do selection and Obsidian export work?

Selection depends on your host: in-conversation choice cards or numbered replies are supported. Obsidian archiving requires a destination you provide and local file access.

Will it publish my work automatically?

No. Generating or saving a report does not publish it. This public repository contains generic skill instructions and fictional examples, not real activity records.
